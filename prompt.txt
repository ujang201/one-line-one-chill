Write one single-sentence horror line, under 200 characters, that frightens a reader in one breath. It must read as a complete horror story in one sentence: concrete and vivid, with a specific image a reader can see, an original turn no one has met a hundred times before (no mirror, doll, or basement), and a tight rhythm whose last words land the blow. The scare must be earned, not announced — dread that outlasts the line, built from what is withheld as much as what is shown. Gore for its own sake does not count. Output only the line itself, nothing else.
